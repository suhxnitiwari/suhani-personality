    Playfair Display

    A high-contrast serif with roots in 18th-century type: dramatic thick-and-thin strokes that still feel timeless. That's the creative traditionalist: bold, expressive, and classic all at once. The heavy weight is her strong spirit. The sweeping italic is her soft heart.

    Headlines · titles · italics
    Aa

    DM Sans

    A clean, friendly geometric sans with soft round shapes. It's easy to read and never cold. That's the ESFJ host: organized, structured, and making everyone feel comfortable. It's the steady hand that keeps the drama readable.
